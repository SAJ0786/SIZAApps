Community Businesses Australia

Discover community businesses through the same connected ecosystem.

Community Businesses Australia is being developed as an integrated business directory inside Community Events Australia. It will help users discover businesses, services and promotions while giving business owners tools to manage their own listings.

Business discoverySearch by business, category, city and open-now status
Rich profilesContact details, WhatsApp, websites, hours, addresses and imagery
Owner centreCreate, edit and resubmit listings with approval status
Trusted listingsPlanned server-side ABN and authority verification
PromotionsOwner-created offers with approval and expiry controls
AnalyticsPlanned insights for views, calls, directions and engagement
Our vision

One community. One connected platform.

We see community technology as more than a list of events or businesses. Our goal is to create useful connections between people, organisers, venues, services and local businesses.

As the ecosystem grows, events can connect with sponsors, vendors and suppliers, while users can move naturally from discovering a program to finding the services around it.

Built for communities

Simple. Secure. Connected.

Community Events Australia has evolved from a community calendar into a broader platform supporting organisers and users across multiple Australian cities.

  • Multi-city discovery across major Australian cities
  • Map view, directions and location-aware discovery
  • Recurring events and prayer-time-based scheduling
  • Hijri calendar and important Shia date support
  • Push notifications, email reminders and calendar feeds
  • Livestreaming and archived stream support
  • Admin tools, permissions and privacy controls
