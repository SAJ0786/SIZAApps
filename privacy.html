Privacy Policy

How SIZA Apps handles information across Community Events Australia and Community Businesses Australia.

Updated 8 September 2026Document draft-2026-09-08-community-connect
Pre-launch draft. This consolidated policy requires Australian legal review before a public store launch. The production provider inventory, retention periods, age position and overseas-processing disclosures must be confirmed.

Operator and scope

Community Connect Australia is operated by Sajjad Zaheer in New South Wales, Australia. SIZA Apps is the product and initiative name. This policy covers Community Connect Australia and its Community Events Australia and Community Businesses Australia modules, including Android and iOS apps, associated websites, administration tools and support channels.

Website: siza.info. Privacy and support email: support@siza.info. We may need to verify an access, correction, account-closure, security or complaint request before acting.

Information we handle

  • Account: verified mobile number, name, optional email, role, default city, module preferences and consent records.
  • Events: event, organiser and host details, posters, addresses, dates, speakers/reciters, stream links and management history.
  • Directory: restricted listing-owner data, optional ABN, declarations, addresses and contact routing, plus the separately sanitised public business profile selected by the owner.
  • Communications: Contact Host, Contact Business, feedback, reports, support correspondence and moderation history.
  • Device and activity: notification status, locally scheduled reminders, location when requested, calendar actions, app version/build and privacy-filtered diagnostics.

Do not submit passwords, verification codes, financial details, sensitive identity documents or unnecessary personal information. The initial release does not collect payment data.

Why information is used

Information is used to operate accounts; publish and administer submitted events, listings and promotions; route messages; provide search, maps, nearby sorting, reminders, calendars and streaming; prevent misuse; moderate content; send service email; respond to support; meet legal obligations; and maintain reliability. Information is not sold.

Public and restricted business records

Restricted business submissions are separated from public directory records. Public listings exclude owner UID, private contact routing, ABN number, declarations, moderation data and exact home/mobile-service addresses unless the owner intentionally chooses a public storefront address. An ABN badge represents only the recorded ABN status/name check.

Location, notifications and calendars

Location is optional and requested when using features such as Near me. Users can browse by city without location access. Notifications and calendar access are controlled by device permissions and app preferences. Local reminders may be disabled in Profile and device settings.

Processors and disclosure

Necessary information may be processed by providers including Google Firebase and Google Cloud, Google Maps and Places, Amazon Web Services Simple Email Service, Expo/EAS, Apple, Google Play, YouTube and configured video-streaming technology. These providers and subprocessors may process data in Australia, the United States or other countries. Service email is sent from and replies to support@siza.info.

Retention, archives and account closure

The service uses restricted archives for events, business listings, promotions, users, reports, moderation decisions and audit records. Account closure disables authentication and removes public access while marking the profile and calendar subscription inactive rather than erasing historical records. Public projections are removed when underlying records are archived or marked deleted. Authorised Super Admin access is limited to safety, audit, restoration, disputes and repeat-submission eligibility review.

Security, children and complaints

Safeguards include role-based access, public/private record separation, restricted credentials, privacy-filtered diagnostics and audit history. The final policy must confirm age and guardian requirements. Report a privacy or security concern to support@siza.info.
