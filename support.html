SIZA Apps support

How can we help?

Support for Community Connect Australia, Community Events Australia and Community Businesses Australia.

Contact support

support@siza.info

Include the product/module, phone model, Android or iOS version, app version/build, approximate time and a short description. A screenshot can help if it does not expose private information.

Never send a password, SMS verification code, stream key, financial information or unnecessary identity document.

Report a technical problem

  1. Open Menu → Help & Policies.
  2. Copy the Diagnostic Session ID.
  3. Send it with what happened and the approximate time.

The ID lets authorised administrators find privacy-filtered events for that device/session in the Diagnostics Register. It is not a password and does not provide remote access to your phone.
